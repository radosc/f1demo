Example mod for Fallout Demo HD

proto/items/00040.json             Stimpak heals 20 hit points instead of 12 (a copy of
                                   data/proto/items/00040.json with "amount" changed).
text/english/dialog/rock.msg       The gate guard greets you with a different line. Message
                                   files in mods only need the lines they change.

Enable it by removing the "-" in front of "example-mod" in mods/mods.txt, then start the game.
